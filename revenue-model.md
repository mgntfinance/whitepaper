# Revenue Model

Magnet Finance generates revenue from three distinct sources. Each source has a specific character — how it's generated, how fast it compounds, and how it responds to protocol growth. That character determines which token it routes to.

## Revenue Stream 1: Swap Fees

**Source:** Magnetic Swap — Jupiter v6 API integration  
**Fee:** 30bps (0.30%) platform fee on every swap  
**Destination:** Treasury, then distributed via swap fee amplifier to $MGNT holders

Swap fees are the protocol's most immediate and transaction-driven revenue source. Every time a user swaps through mgntfinance.com/swap, 30bps of the swap value flows to the Magnet Finance treasury.

This is routed to $MGNT holders via the polarity amplifier — because $MGNT is the core utility token, it earns from the core protocol utility action.

**Current status:** Live on mainnet. Jupiter referral account: `3zLDqUpbeRSUT7WhzL7pnVe7jCyPW5g8MFKJmv5MVP8y`

## Revenue Stream 2: LP Yield

**Source:** Meteora DLMM concentrated liquidity pool (mgntSOL/SOL)  
**Mechanism:** Trading fees generated by the DLMM pool as users swap between mgntSOL and SOL  
**Destination:** Distributed via LP yield amplifier to pMGNT holders

LP yield is volume-driven. When the mgntSOL/SOL pool sees high trading volume, LP yield increases. pMGNT — the positive polarity, volume-oriented token — earns from this stream because it aligns token character with revenue character.

**Current status:** Live on mainnet. Pool: `8j7Sk4Q8LcnadMf9T9TRBYePhJaouxi5K2tx3ZQRXDYo`

## Revenue Stream 3: Treasury Yield

**Source:** Diversified yield positions deployed from presale proceeds  
**Mechanism:** External lending protocols, LP positions, and yield strategies  
**Destination:** Distributed via treasury yield amplifier to nMGNT holders

Treasury yield is the slow-compounding, long-duration stream. It requires patient capital and long time horizons to maximize. nMGNT — the negative polarity, scarcity token — earns from this stream because its holders are by design the most patient, long-conviction participants in the ecosystem.

**Current status:** Pending presale completion. Treasury yield positions are deployed once presale capital is secured.

## Distribution Architecture

All three revenue streams feed into the **Weekly Magnetic Epoch** for distribution.

```
Protocol Revenue
       │
       ├─── Base Layer (fixed %) ──────────────────► All holders proportionally
       │
       └─── Amplifier Pool (remaining)
               │
               ├─── Swap Fee Revenue ─────────────► $MGNT polarity amplifier
               ├─── LP Yield ────────────────────► pMGNT polarity amplifier
               └─── Treasury Yield ──────────────► nMGNT polarity amplifier
```

## Burn Integration

A portion of every epoch's revenue funds $MGNT buybacks on the open market. Those $MGNT tokens are then burned on-chain — reducing circulating supply permanently.

Combined with pMGNT's 0.5% transfer burn and nMGNT's 1.5% transfer burn, all three token supplies compress simultaneously every week.

## Protocol Sustainability

The model is designed to be self-reinforcing:

* More SOL staked → deeper mgntSOL liquidity → higher LP yield → stronger pMGNT amplifier
* More swap volume → higher swap fees → stronger $MGNT amplifier
* More presale capital deployed → higher treasury yield → stronger nMGNT amplifier

Growth in any dimension strengthens at least one revenue stream. Growth across all dimensions strengthens all three.
