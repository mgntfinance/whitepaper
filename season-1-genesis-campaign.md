## How to Earn Points

### Hold mgntSOL — Passive Accrual

The primary earning method. Simply hold mgntSOL in your wallet and earn points automatically.

**Formula:** `mgntSOL balance × 10 pts/hr`

- Balances scanned every 5 minutes
- No minimum balance required
- Points displayed to 3 decimal places, no rounding
- Accumulates 24/7 with zero active management

### Swap Volume — Magnetic Swap

Swap tokens through [mgntfinance.com/swap](https://mgntfinance.com/swap) to earn additional points based on volume.
