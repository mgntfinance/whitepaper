# Season 1 Genesis Campaign

Season 1 is Magnet Finance's foundation-building phase. Before $MGNT launches for trading and before pMGNT and nMGNT mint in Season 2, Season 1 establishes the community, the leaderboard, and the holders who will be first in line for protocol rewards.

## What Season 1 Is

Season 1 is a **points-based early participation campaign** built around two core actions:

1. **Hold mgntSOL** — stake SOL and hold the liquid staking token
2. **Swap on Magnetic Swap** — use the live swap hub at mgntfinance.com/swap

Both actions earn Genesis Points that will translate directly into future protocol rewards.

## Points Formula

### mgntSOL Holding

```
Points = mgntSOL balance × 10 pts/hr
```

* Calculated to 3 decimal places (no rounding)
* Balances scanned every 5 minutes via Supabase
* Points accrue continuously while you hold

### Magnetic Swap Volume

Swap volume on Magnetic Swap contributes additional points. Details are displayed on the Season 1 leaderboard at mgntfinance.com/swap.

## The Leaderboard

The Season 1 Genesis leaderboard is **live** at mgntfinance.com/swap. Rankings update in near real-time as balances are scanned and swap volume is recorded.

All point balances are stored on-chain-verifiable infrastructure (Supabase with pg_cron) and are publicly auditable.

## Why Participate Early

Season 1 participants are the genesis community — the holders who built positions before the protocol was fully public, before $MGNT launched for trading, and before the polarity amplifier system activated.

Their Genesis Points represent the earliest and most significant community commitment, and the rewards structure will reflect that.
