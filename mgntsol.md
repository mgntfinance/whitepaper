# mgntSOL — Liquid Staking

mgntSOL is Magnet Finance's liquid staking token. Stake SOL through the protocol, receive mgntSOL in return, and remain fully liquid across the Solana DeFi ecosystem — without sacrificing native Solana staking yield.

**Mint:** `mgntr7GPnAM6g5cDYMAmjbLvNmbfG3cN1pjAvbnWVKK`

## How It Works

When you stake SOL through Magnet Finance, you receive mgntSOL at a 1:1 ratio (adjusting over time as yield accrues). Your SOL is delegated to Solana validators, generating native staking yield. That yield is reflected in the mgntSOL/SOL exchange rate over time — your mgntSOL becomes worth progressively more SOL.

You can unstake at any time through the Sanctum interface, or use mgntSOL directly across Solana DeFi without unstaking at all.
