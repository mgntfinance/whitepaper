## Choosing Your Polarity

When you stake $MGNT in Season 2, you choose which derivative to receive. This is not a sacrifice — staking is always additive:

| You Hold | You Earn |
|----------|----------|
| $MGNT (unstaked) | Base layer + swap fee amplifier |
| $MGNT staked → pMGNT | Base layer + swap fee amplifier + LP yield amplifier |
| $MGNT staked → nMGNT | Base layer + swap fee amplifier + treasury yield amplifier |

The choice is about *how* you want to earn more — not whether you earn.
