# Token Architecture

Magnet Finance operates a tri-token system where each token has a distinct character, revenue position, and deflationary mechanic. All three supplies compress simultaneously.

**Value by Volume.**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Max Supply | 55,350,000 |
| Transfer Fee | 1% (0.5% burn + 0.5% rewards vault) |
| Revenue | Base layer + LP yield amplifier |

pMGNT is the volume-oriented token. Holders earn from LP yield — the revenue stream driven by swap volume through the Meteora DLMM pool. The more volume flows through Magnetic Swap and the mgntSOL/SOL pool, the more LP yield is generated, and the more pMGNT holders earn.

Every pMGNT transfer burns 0.5% of the transferred amount and routes 0.5% to the rewards vault, funding future distributions.

## nMGNT — Negative Polarity

**Value by Scarcity.**

> *Minting begins in Season 2*

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|

| Transfer Fee | 2% (1.5% burn + 0.5% treasury) |

nMGNT is the scarcity token — the most aggressively deflationary asset in the ecosystem. Every transfer burns 1.5% of the amount, making nMGNT's circulating supply compress faster than any other token in the system.

Holders earn from treasury yield — the revenue generated from diversified long-duration yield positions. This aligns nMGNT's character (patience, long-term conviction) with its revenue source (slow-compounding treasury returns).

**Color:** Red `#FF3D00`

## Simultaneous Burn Mechanics

All three token supplies compress at the same time, through different mechanisms:

| Token | Burn Mechanism | Frequency |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| $MGNT | Weekly buybacks from swap fee revenue, burned on-chain | Weekly (Magnetic Epoch) |
| pMGNT | 0.5% burn on every transfer | Every transfer |
| nMGNT | 1.5% burn on every transfer | Every transfer |

The result: regardless of which token you hold, your share of a declining total supply increases over time.
