# Magnetic Polarity Revenue Routing

Magnetic Polarity Revenue Routing is the core innovation of Magnet Finance. Revenue is not pooled and distributed generically — it is routed by *character*, matching each income stream to the token archetype that earned it.

This is what separates Magnet Finance from every other DeFi protocol: the mechanic doesn't just reward participation, it rewards *conviction*. Your token choice is an expression of how you want to earn.

---

## Three Revenue Streams

Magnet Finance generates revenue from three distinct sources, each with a different economic character:

| Stream | Source | Character |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Swap Fees** | Magnetic Swap (Jupiter v6, 30bps platform fee) | High-frequency, volume-driven |
| **LP Yield** | Meteora DLMM concentrated liquidity (mgntSOL/SOL) | Continuous, position-driven |
| **Treasury Yield** | Diversified yield positions funded by presale proceeds | Long-duration, compounding |

---

## Two-Tier Distribution — The Weekly Magnetic Epoch

Every 7 days, all protocol revenue is tallied across the three streams and distributed on-chain in two tiers. Every transaction is publicly verifiable.

> **Note:** "Magnetic Epoch" refers to Magnet Finance's weekly distribution cycle — not Solana's native 2–3 day validator epoch.

### Tier 1 — Base Layer

A fixed percentage of total protocol revenue distributes **proportionally to ALL $MGNT, pMGNT, and nMGNT holders** every epoch, regardless of which token they hold or how long they've held it.

**No token holder earns zero. Every level of participation is rewarded.**

### Tier 2 — Polarity Amplifiers

The remaining revenue routes by source to the matching token polarity:

| Token | Amplifier Source | Why It Matches |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| **$MGNT** | Swap fee revenue | Core utility token earns from core protocol activity |
| **pMGNT** | LP yield | Positive polarity / volume token earns from volume-driven yield |
| **nMGNT** | Treasury yield | Negative polarity / scarcity token earns from long-duration compounding |

---

## Staking Is Always Double-Up, Never a Sacrifice

This is a core protocol design principle. Staking $MGNT to receive pMGNT or nMGNT is **always additive**. Holders never lose their $MGNT earnings position by staking.

| Position | Earnings |
|----------|----------|
| Unstaked $MGNT | Base layer + swap fee amplifier |
| Staked $MGNT (→ pMGNT or nMGNT) | Base layer + swap fee amplifier + polarity amplifier |

Lock duration multiplies amplifier weight. Earnings vest only if staked through the full weekly epoch snapshot. **The longer you lock, the more you earn — and you never give anything up to do it.**

---

## Burn Mechanics

All three token supplies compress simultaneously, every epoch:

| Token | Burn Source |
|-------|-------------|
| **$MGNT** | Weekly buybacks funded by a portion of swap fee revenue, burned on-chain |
| **pMGNT** | 0.5% burn on every transfer |
| **nMGNT** | 1.5% burn on every transfer |

The result: regardless of which token you hold, the total supply of the Magnet Finance ecosystem is always declining.
